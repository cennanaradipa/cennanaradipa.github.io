Welcome 
=====

Hi, Cenna here! :smile:

I am a Postdoctoral researcher working at the Singapore Synchrotron Light Source. My research focus is on condensed matter physics and material science. Most of my experience is in spectroscopic ellipsometry, X-ray synchrotron methods, and density functional theory calculations. 

I finished my post-graduate studies in National University of Singapore and my undergraduate studies at Universitas Indonesia. This website contains a summary of my recent publications, [which you can check out here](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=MHOCWm0AAAAJ&hl=en), and a couple of other notes as well.

Teaching
=======

I had experience with tutoring high school students with IB diploma (online and in-person) when I was in Jakarta, teaching English lessons for TOEFL preparation, and also taught some piano lessons during my undergraduate studies.

I'm open for online lessons or discussions on these topics. Please reach out too if you're part of non-profit organization or project.
